Scoring

Deterministic, on chain, explainable

base 300
+ min(repays, 40) × 6
+ min(bitlen(repaid USD), 12) × 8
+ clamp(repaid ÷ borrowed, 0, 1) × 80
+ min(weeks since first fact, 24) × 4
+ recency (30 d: 40 · 90 d: 20)
+ min(native repays, 10) × 5
− liquidations (90, +45 each, +60 recent)
− defaults × 120
clamp 300 … 850

Tiers

Bronze <500 · Silver 500–649 · Gold 650–749 · Platinum ≥750
